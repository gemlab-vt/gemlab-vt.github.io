Generative worlds. Human direction.

Generative Modeling (GEM) Lab is led by Dr. Pinar Yanardag in the Department of Computer Science at Virginia Tech. We build generative models for images, video, and interactive worlds that follow human direction: controllable at a fine-grained level, personal to the concepts you care about, and efficient enough to run over long horizons.
