GEnerative Modeling (GEM) Lab

GEnerative Modeling Lab.

GEMLAB is led by Dr. Pinar Yanardag at Virginia Tech, Department of Computer Science. We research generative models — controllable image and video generation, diffusion-based editing and personalization, creativity, and the interpretability of modern generative systems.

01Research thrusts

Research

Three threads run through our work on generative models.

Thrust 01

Discovering & Leveraging Latent Capabilities for Efficient Control

We uncover and exploit the internal representations of generative models to enable efficient, training-free control of image and video synthesis — from fine-grained controllable edits to the temporal domain through video and motion editing, dynamic view synthesis, and long-horizon autoregressive video generation.

CONFORMFluxSpaceNoiseCLRRAVEMotionFlowInverse DVS∞-RoPE
Thrust 02

Integrating Cross-Modal Capabilities for Advanced Generation

We bridge vision and language, unifying the reasoning of VLMs and LLMs with the generative priors of visual models to unlock tasks beyond either modality alone — agentic frameworks, multi-modal explainability, and reinforcement-learning methods for image and video synthesis.

CREADiffExC-DPODPP-GRPO
Thrust 03

Personalized, Human-Aligned & Democratized Generative AI

We move beyond one-size-fits-all generative AI with methods for personalized content generation, and techniques that democratize generative AI for creators through accessible, intuitive creative control.
